# Vicu 2.6.0 for Android and iOS

Vicu 2.6.0 brings guided organizing to the phone: sessions that take you through your Inbox, overdue tasks and projects one decision at a time. You can also choose what each swipe does, switch the dark theme to pure black, hide the drawer views you do not use and start dictating from the app icon. Update the desktop app to 2.6.0 at the same time.

### Guided organizing

- **One task at a time.** Vicu picks what needs a decision and shows one card at a time. Give the task a date, move it to another project, change its title, complete it, delete it or skip it, then go on with Next; Back shows the previous card. At the end a summary says what changed, with **Undo all**.
- **Four sessions:** Sort the Inbox, Sort out overdue, Waiting a long time (undated tasks nobody touched for 60 days or more) and Review projects. Start them from the Inbox's top bar, the Review screen or Ask.
- **Project review** shows each project due for review with a health line, its untouched tasks and its next step. Keep or archive it, then mark it reviewed.
- **Weekly review**, on the Review screen: a checklist that takes you through the Inbox, anything on your mind, the past week, overdue tasks, what is coming up, your projects and what has waited too long. Finish records the review and syncs it to your other devices.
- **Works without the assistant.** With Ask set up, the model suggests a decision for each card and says why; you still decide. An empty Ask conversation offers these sessions too.

### Swipes

- **Choose what each swipe does**, in Settings, Gestures: Complete, Choose when, Due today, Due tomorrow, Mark urgent, Move to project, Delete or Nothing, for a swipe right and a swipe left. The defaults are unchanged: right completes, left opens When. If you had set the swipe to Urgent, the left swipe stays Urgent.
- Delete asks first while "Confirm before deleting" is on, and always for a task with subtasks. Move to project takes the subtasks along.

### Pure black

- **Pure black**, in Settings, General, Appearance, makes the dark theme's backgrounds black, for OLED screens and night use. Sheets, menus, the drawer and the bottom bar stay a step lighter. It is offered unless the theme is Light. The home-screen widgets keep their own colours. ([#57](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/57))

### Drawer

- **Hide the views you do not use.** Settings, General, Drawer has a switch for Today, Upcoming and Anytime (while the bottom bar does not hold them), Logbook, Routines and Review (while they are on) and Ask (once it is set up). A hidden view keeps working: reminders, widgets and notifications are unchanged. This is set per device.

### Voice

- **Dictate from the app icon.** Hold the app icon for **Add task** and **Dictate**, on Android and iOS. Dictate opens the app listening: with smart dictation set up it opens the dictation sheet for the Inbox, otherwise the voice card. On Android, "Add by voice" becomes Dictate; a copy you pinned to the home screen keeps working.
- **Android widget mic with your speech model.** With a speech model chosen in Settings, Assistant, Hearing, the widgets' mic listens in a card over the home screen with that model, so you can say the task in any language the model knows. The first time it asks for the microphone. Without a model it uses the system speech screen, as before.

### Other changes

- **Custom list icons** are chosen from a grid of the icons themselves instead of a list of names. The bottom bar's icon choice uses the same grid, with larger touch targets. ([#54](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/54))
- **Sessions in Vikunja.** Vikunja's Settings, Sessions now names Vicu and the device, for example "Vicu/2.6.0 (Android; Google Pixel 10 Pro)", instead of "ktor-client". A session from before keeps its old name until that device signs in again. ([#56](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/56))

### Fixes

- **Street addresses** of saved places now follow the country of the place: "123 Main Street" in the US, the UK, Australia and the other countries that put the number first, "Hoofdstraat 123" elsewhere. ([#55](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/55))
