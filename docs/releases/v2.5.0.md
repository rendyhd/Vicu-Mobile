# Vicu 2.5.0 for Android and iOS

Vicu 2.5.0 adds an assistant that runs on the AI model you choose: talk and your tasks appear as cards, organize a long brain dump into projects and tasks, and ask about your tasks by voice. Speech can now be recognized on the phone itself, and projects can be grouped under categories. Update the desktop app to 2.5.0 at the same time: it has the same assistant and categories.

### An assistant with your own model

- **Off until you set it up**, in Settings, Assistant. Choose a provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama, Open WebUI or any OpenAI-compatible server), paste your key and pick a model; Vicu tests the model as soon as you choose it. There is no Vicu server: requests go from your phone straight to your provider, with your key. Before anything goes to a cloud provider, Vicu shows what each feature sends and asks you to agree. Each feature in Settings says where to find it and has **Try it**.
- **Quick capture stays as it was.** Hold + and let go, and what you said becomes one task, without the model.
- **Smart dictation.** Hold +, slide up to the sparkle and keep talking. Each pause turns what you said into task cards with dates, projects, labels, priority, reminders and steps, and a dashed outline marks what Vicu guessed. Say what to change ("make the first one Friday", "and call mum tomorrow") and the cards follow. Add adds them with one Undo. The sheet starts small and grows when the cards come. Also **Break into steps** in a task's menu, and **Make tasks** when you share text to Vicu.
- **Brain dump.** Turn on Brain dump at the top of the dictation sheet and talk, or type, for as long as you like: nothing is sent until you tap **Organize**. Everything you said is then sorted into tasks with notes, subtasks and priority, in your projects or in **new projects and subprojects** where none of yours fit. Rename or remove a new project, change a card, or say or type a change and tap **Update**. **Add all** creates the new projects and then the tasks, with one Undo. New projects need a connection.
- **Ask.** The sparkle next to Search on any list opens Ask about that list. It starts small and grows with the first answer, and answers briefly. Changes come as cards that you apply or decline, with Undo. A conversation left for 30 minutes makes way for a new one.
- **Talk.** Turn on the microphone in Ask and just talk: a pause sends it, the answer is read aloud, and it keeps listening. Talk again to interrupt. A change is read out as a yes-or-no question you answer by voice. With headphones it keeps listening while it speaks.

### Speech on the phone

- In Settings, Assistant, Hearing, download a speech model and voice input is recognized on the phone, without sending audio anywhere. **Parakeet** is the one for phones: it keeps up as you talk. Whisper's larger models are made for computers and are slow on a phone. The phone's own recognizer stays the default.
- Downloads use Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data. On Android, speech on the phone needs a 64-bit processor from about 2018 or later.

### Project categories

- Optional named headers that group your top-level projects in the menu, in Settings, Projects and in the project pickers. Use **Add category above** or **Move to category…** in a project's menu; rename, move or delete a category (with Undo) from its header, or drag a project past a header into that category. Collapsed categories show how many projects they hold.
- Categories sync with your other devices and the desktop app. Without any, the project list is as before.

### Other changes

- Pull the task editor down by its header, or past the top of its content, to close it.

### Fixes

- **Android widget:** ticking a routine on the Routines widget hid the row, showed it again for a moment and hid it once more. It now stays hidden.
- **Android routine reminders:** a routine ticked on another device, or ticked earlier on this phone, could still ring later. Every routine reminder now checks the routine on the server first; offline, the phone's copy decides.
