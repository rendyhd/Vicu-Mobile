# Vicu 2.6.2 for Android and iOS

Vicu 2.6.2 lets you duplicate tasks, copy the titles of a selection and assign people to several tasks at once. Today keeps its routine section short, projects and lists can go without an icon, and you can play the completion sound before keeping it. Update the desktop app to 2.6.2 at the same time: it has the same changes.

### New

- **Duplicate a task.** Duplicate is in the task's menu and in the selection's menu. The copy is a new open task in the same project with the title, notes, dates, priority, repeat, reminders, labels, assignees and subtasks. It works offline, and from the task's menu the copy opens right away.
- **Copy titles.** Select tasks and choose **Copy titles** in the menu to put their titles on the clipboard, one per line, for pasting outside Vicu.
- **Assign several tasks at once.** When every selected task is in a shared project, the selection's menu has **Assign…**. A person assigned to all of them is ticked, to some of them partly ticked; pick someone to assign them to every selected task, or a ticked person to take them off all of them.
- **A shorter routine section on Today.** Today shows one row per routine, at its next time, with how far you are (such as 1/3) for a routine with several times. Only the routines due within two hours, or still open from earlier, show: two by default, the rest behind one line such as "2 later today". Tap the Routines header to fold the section. Choose how many show in Settings, General, **Routines shown on Today**.
- **No icon.** Edit Project has **Folder** or **None**, and a custom list's icon picker has **None**, for names that start with an emoji. With None the name starts where the icon would be. The choice syncs to the desktop app.
- **Play the completion sound.** Settings has a Play button next to the completion sound, so you can hear a sound you picked before keeping it. A file that cannot be played says so.

### Fixes

- **Assistant addresses.** Switching provider no longer wipes the address you typed for Other: each provider keeps its address and models. Other, Ollama and Open WebUI keep up to five saved addresses, each with its own models and key, and **Add address** saves another one. The address field says that the version path (such as /v1) is needed, and an error from an address without one says it may be missing.
