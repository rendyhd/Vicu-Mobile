# Vicu 2.6.1 for Android and iOS

Vicu 2.6.1 lets you sign in with an identity provider that asks for a passkey, such as Pocket ID. Update the desktop app to 2.6.1 at the same time: it does the same.

### Fixes

- **Sign in with a passkey.** Identity providers that sign in with a passkey, such as Pocket ID, could not finish in Vicu's sign-in page: it ended with "An unknown error occurred" or "canceled or timed out". Vicu now notices the passkey request and offers to sign in through your browser, where passkeys work. **Uses a passkey? Sign in through your browser** is also on the sign-in screen.
- The browser sign-in needs one setting at your provider: add the redirect address the sign-in screen shows (it has a Copy button) to Vikunja's client there.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/compare/v2.6.0...v2.6.1
